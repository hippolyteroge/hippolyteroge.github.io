Who I am

I got drawn to financial markets very quickly: I wanted to learn to invest, to grow my savings. Like everyone, I started with a huge ego, and the market took real pleasure in destroying it. I lost my entire capital several times, repeating the same mistakes.

I got burned by signal groups and trading bots, because I thought trading was simple enough to hand the responsibility off to someone or something else, but too complicated for me: unable to analyze an asset properly, or with any confidence. I tried pretty much everything you can try, without ever really understanding what I was looking at.

Except life doesn't hand you anything: you're the only one responsible for your results. Nothing else should depend on it for you to invest and sleep soundly.

Ichimoku changed that. It's a simple indicator to master that gives a clear view of the trend and every technical level at a glance, exactly what every previous method was missing.

I don't have a finance degree. A vocational cooking diploma, that's it. Everything I eventually learned, I learned through experience and results, not theory.

Today, after 7 years of trading, I have a routine in place and a dashboard to track everything, to do things properly, without exception. I trade from Thailand, where I moved thanks to it.

The method

I rely on Ichimoku, not the simplified version you find everywhere, but a complete read: trend, technical levels, and reversal zones, visible at a glance on any asset.

Most indicators only answer one question: RSI measures momentum, Bollinger Bands measure volatility, FVGs measure imbalance zones. None of them tells you, on its own, where the trend is, where the levels that matter are, and where the market is likely to reverse. Ichimoku answers all three at once. That's why once you know how to read it, you stop stacking indicators.

The details (how I structure a trading plan with it, step by step) are the core of the coaching, not something I can sum up in three lines here.

Whether you're structuring active trades (scalping, intraday, swing) to aim for your prop firm funding, or building a long-term portfolio, the principle stays the same: read the trend and the levels before acting, not after.
